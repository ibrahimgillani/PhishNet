    Our Story

    PhishNet is an AI-powered cybersecurity solution designed to protect users from phishing websites and malicious emails in real time. Built as a lightweight browser-based system, PhishNet combines heuristic analysis with machine learning models to detect deceptive links, fraudulent websites, and phishing emails before they cause harm. Our goal is to make cybersecurity accessible to everyone by providing clear, non-technical warnings that help users make safer decisions online. By integrating directly into the browsing and email experience, PhishNet delivers seamless, privacy-focused protection that reduces human error and strengthens digital trust for both individuals and organizations.
